# Reviewer Risk Register

This register treats likely reviewer objections as design constraints. It is not intended for inclusion verbatim in the main manuscript.

| Reviewer concern | Current evidence | Manuscript response | Residual risk | Action before submission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The synthetic LabGold benchmark is too easy | Primary PaddleOCR observed zero errors at 100% coverage | State this prominently as a negative result and do not claim superiority | High | Add preregistered zero-shot ClinOCR-Bench external OCR generalization |
| SafeOCR reduces coverage without demonstrating benefit over the primary OCR engine | SafeOCR coverage 72.57%; primary OCR perfect on LabGold | Frame v0.1 contribution as verification contract and governed evidence, not accuracy improvement | High | External stress evidence; future field-level real-world annotations |
| Independent OCR engines may make correlated errors | PaddleOCR and Tesseract are architecturally different but not statistically independent | Use "independent OCR-family reread" operationally, not probabilistic independence | Medium | Add explicit limitation and future multi-engine correlation study |
| Tesseract crop baseline inherits field location | F6 artifact explicitly records this limitation | Label its accuracy as crop-read correctness, not full extraction | Low | Keep limitation adjacent to baseline result |
| Pixel provenance is an implementation detail rather than research contribution | EvidenceRecord makes source binding a mandatory admission condition | Explain provenance as an operational safety constraint, not a logging feature | Medium | Figure 1 + one evidence-trace example |
| Existing source-grounded trust gates make SafeOCR incremental | Girda & Groza 2026 overlaps source verification, same-row evidence, provenance and review | Narrow novelty to the combined OCR verification contract, independent reread, perturbation stability, criticality, patient linkage, governed risk/coverage, gated FHIR | High | Keep direct comparison table and avoid "first" claims |
| Verified abstention is not new | Ben Hmida et al. 2025 and selective prediction literature establish abstention | Cite directly and claim integration/evaluation rather than invention | Low | Retain novelty map |
| The study lacks a formal risk guarantee | v0.1 reports Wilson intervals, not conformal guarantees | State explicitly; compare with Learn-then-Test/conformal verification | Medium | Position formal risk control as next study |
| Zero observed unsafe accepts may be misread as zero risk | Wilson upper bound 1.805% | Always pair zero observed errors with the interval | Low | Automated claim audit before submission |
| ClinOCR-Bench transcript truth cannot evaluate SafeOCR field safety | Benchmark provides full-document human-audited transcripts | Use only for OCR generalization; withhold field safety claims | Low | Preserve preregistered interpretation boundary |
| FHIR validator success could be mistaken for clinical mapping correctness | Validator artifact has zero errors; F6 field mapping rate is non-estimable | Report conformance and mapping correctness as separate claims | Low | Keep mapping error as not estimable |
| No real-world multi-site validation | Current public evidence is synthetic/public PHI-free | Describe paper as preclinical/offline | High | Future multi-site independently annotated study |
| No clinician human-factors evaluation | v0.1 emits REVIEW_REQUIRED but has no review workflow | Declare out of scope | High for clinical journals | Target methodology journal; future DECIDE-AI-stage study |
| No fairness analysis | Public benchmark lacks meaningful patient-demographic labels for this claim | Do not fabricate subgroup fairness results | Medium | State non-estimable and design future stratified study |
| Patient attribution metric may be artificially easy | Synthetic benchmark construction limits ambiguity | Do not generalize to real multi-patient workflows | Medium | Future identity-conflict stress set |
| Row association may be easier in synthetic templates | OCR-only association scorer observed no errors | Treat as benchmark-specific evidence only | Medium | External table-layout evaluation |
| Reproducibility is weak if evaluation depends on mutable models | Exact versions, model names, hashes, split hashes and heads are recorded | Provide release manifest and frozen evidence artifacts | Low | Verify public release URLs before submission |
| Post-hoc tuning could contaminate external evaluation | ClinOCR protocol and evaluator were committed before opening evaluation images/transcripts | Report exact preregistration commits and frozen config | Low | Preserve original run and any failure/resume evidence |
| Paper overstates medical AI relevance because there is no decision support | System transforms clinical documents but does not diagnose/treat | Position as clinical informatics / data quality infrastructure | Low | Prefer JBI-style methodology framing |
| Results are too narrow for a high-impact general clinical journal | v0.1 targets lab reports and offline evidence | Do not inflate scope | High | Submit where methodology and reproducibility are valued |
